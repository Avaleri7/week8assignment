Three food trucks in Philadelphia that are worth checking out!

Food Trucks I Love

Philadelphia has many food trucks with different types of food. Here are three food trucks I enjoy or would like to try.

My Three Favorite Food Trucks
Food Truck Name Truck Location Truck Photo Food Type Why It's My Favorite
El Sabor de Puebla Olney, Philadelphia, across from Compare & Save Mexican Food I love their birria tacos, and their horchata is honestly one of my favorite drinks.
El Zurdo Hunting Park, Philadelphia Dominican Food I've only tried their yaroa made with French fries, but it was delicious. I'd definitely order it again.
The Crepe Truck Philly Philadelphia, PA Desserts I would like to try their sweet crepes because I enjoy desserts and different sweet combinations.
